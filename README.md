# Dompetara

Dompetara helps you keep track of your money across bank accounts, cash, and e-wallets. Record income and spending, move money between wallets, and see where your money goes.

[Open Dompetara](https://personal-ledger-inky-alpha.vercel.app)

## Getting started

1. Sign in with your Google account.
2. Create your first wallet and enter its opening balance.
3. Add income, expenses, or transfers from **Transactions**.
4. Visit **Report** to explore your spending, or **Settings** to manage categories, change your language, and download your records.

## Wallets and transactions

- Keep separate balances in Indonesian rupiah (IDR), US dollars (USD), and Canadian dollars (CAD).
- Track bank accounts, cash, and e-wallets with names you choose.
- Add notes and categories to income and expenses.
- Transfer money between wallets, including across currencies. Enter the amounts actually sent and received, or use a suggested reference exchange rate.
- Record transfer service fees against the wallet that paid them.
- Correct a wallet balance without counting the correction as income or spending.

Editing or deleting a transaction updates the affected wallet balances. Removing a category keeps its name on past transactions.

## Receipts

Choose **Import receipt** in Transactions to read a JPEG or PNG receipt using OCR or AI. Review the merchant, date, wallet, category, and amount before saving.

Save just the total or keep individual items, discounts, taxes, and fees. The receipt becomes one expense, so its items are not counted as separate charges. Payment screenshots require you to confirm that the payment represents spending; money moved between your own wallets should be recorded as a transfer.

Receipt reading can make mistakes, especially with blurry images or hidden details. Always check the result before saving.

## Reports

See income and expenses for a selected month or date range, compare spending by category, and follow daily and monthly spending trends. View balances in their original currencies or as an estimated combined balance.

Reference exchange rates are estimates. Your bank's actual charged or received amount takes precedence.

## Language

Dompetara supports English and Bahasa Indonesia. It starts in Indonesian when that is your preferred browser language; otherwise it starts in English. A language prompt appears the first time you sign in, and you can change your choice later in **Settings → Language**.

Your saved choice takes precedence over browser detection. Wallet names, custom categories, transaction titles, notes, and text printed on receipts stay as you entered them.

## Appearance

Dompetara follows your device’s light or dark appearance by default. Use the sun or moon button in the header to switch themes, or choose **Light theme**, **Dark theme**, or **System setting** in **Settings → Appearance**. Your theme choice is saved in this browser on this device.

## Your records

Your ledger is tied to your Google account. Transactions use your device's timezone, and you can download your records as JSON or CSV from Settings, including notes and saved receipt details.

Receipt images are sent to an external OCR or AI service for processing. Images are temporary unless you choose **Save receipt image** during review. A saved image is private to your account and can be viewed or removed later; removing it keeps the transaction and its receipt details.
